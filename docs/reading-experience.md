# PDF Reader & Session Tracking

Opening a book launches the built-in PDF reader. Loren quietly tracks your reading sessions to power smart recommendations and insights.

- **📖 PDF Reader**: Full PDF reading experience. Pinch to zoom. Page-turn navigation. Loren remembers exactly which page you were on and resumes from there.
- **📍 Reading Position**: Every book stores your last page. Progress percentage is visible on cards. If you're near the end, Loren notices and may say so mark it as read.
- **⏱ Session Tracking**: Each reading session (start → close) is logged: which book, duration, time of day. This data powers the Insights dashboard and Loren's pattern observations.
- **📊 Circulation Desk**: When you open a book, it's "checked out". Loren tracks how long it's been on your desk and gently reminds you if days go by without returning to it.
- **🧭 Ask Loren in Reader**: Loren's chat is accessible while reading. Ask about the book, find passages, get context — without leaving the reader.
- **📝 Reading Notes**: Add notes while reading via Loren ("add note to Dune: this chapter felt rushed") or through the Notes panel on a book's detail page. Notes are searchable.

### Reading Insights Dashboard

These numbers are optional supporting information. They exist to help you notice your own reading, not to create streaks, scores, or pressure to keep pace.


- **Total books & pages** — Total books in library, total pages read, read vs unread breakdown.
- **Genre breakdown** — Horizontal bar chart of genres in your library and how many books belong to each.
- **When you read** — Pie/bar breakdown of your reading sessions by time of day: morning / afternoon / evening / night.
- **Most opened books** — Ranked list of books by total number of opens — useful for spotting what you keep coming back to.
- **Books added (last 6 months)** — Bar chart of how many books you added each month over the past six months.
- **Reading this week** — Total minutes + day-by-day bar chart of reading time across Monday–Sunday.
- **Session stats** — Total reading time, longest single session, number of sessions this month.
- **Reading personality** — A short text paragraph describing your reading style based on genres, pace, and patterns — e.g. "A deep diver who returns to the same few authors repeatedly."
