# The Four Panels

Loren is divided into four main panels accessible from the bottom navigation bar. Each has its own search, layout, and functionality.

- **📚 Library** — All your books. Searchable. Filterable by folder.
- **❤️ Favorites** — Books you've saved. Always one tap away.
- **🏛️ Discover** — Browse your library as a visual catalog or mosaic.
- **⏳ Recall** — Book Trivia and reading games, drawn from your library.
