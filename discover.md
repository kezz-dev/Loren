# Browsing Your Library Differently

Discover shows your library through a different lens — not as a list to manage, but as a collection to explore.

- **🖼 Mosaic View**: A tactile mosaic wall of all your books, arranged in varying cell sizes (large / medium / small). Each cell shows the book's cover or a color fill with an initial. Tap to reveal title, author, and status. Badges show read status (✓) and favorites (♥). A progress bar sits at the bottom of in-progress books.
- **🏛️ Genre Shelves**: Horizontal scrollable shelves, one per genre. Each shelf shows book covers in a row. A header shows genre name + arrow to scroll. Tap any book to open its detail page. Tapping the genre header opens a full sheet with all books in that genre.
- **🔍 Genre Filter**: A filter toggle shows genre pill buttons. Tap one to filter the catalog to a single genre. Active filter shows a pill count badge next to the filter toggle.
- **🔀 Mosaic Shuffle**: A subtle shuffle button at the bottom of the Mosaic view re-randomizes the layout — useful when you want to see a different mix of books "on the wall".

> **INFO:** In Mosaic, tapping a cell reveals the book name overlay. Tapping again opens the book detail sheet. The mosaic is purely visual — no actions are hidden behind long-press. In Genre Shelves, tapping the large genre name opens a bottom sheet listing all books in that genre with their read status.
