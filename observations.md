*In-Session Intelligence*

# Silent Observations

While you're chatting with Loren, she quietly notices patterns in your behavior and adds a short aside after certain responses — never intrusive, always optional. Each type is used at most once per session, and at least 3 messages must pass before another observation can appear.

| Observation | Trigger | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🦉 Night Owl | Trigger: It's past 11pm AND you have 3+ previous night reading sessions recorded. | "You're up late again. Something about the night and books." |
| 🎭 Genre Streak | Trigger: You've asked for the same genre 3+ times within this session's conversation stack. | "You've been in a fantasy mood — that's 3 in a row now." |
| 👤 Author Loyalty | Trigger: You keep searching for the same author AND have opened their books 5+ times total. | "You keep coming back to Ursula Le Guin. 7 opens across their books." |
| 📌 Unread Favorite | Trigger: A result book has been saved to Favorites but never actually opened. | "You saved '[Book]' to favorites a while back — but haven't opened it yet." |
| 🌵 Long Dry Spell | Trigger: No books opened in the past 14+ days (but you have books in your library). | "It's been 18 days since you opened anything. Everything okay?" |
| 🔁 Repeated Opener | Trigger: A result book has been opened 3+ times but has never been marked as finished. | "You've opened '[Book]' 4 times but haven't marked it finished." |

> **WARNING:** Observations are never delivered right after an emotional message (e.g. if you said "I'm feeling sad" in the last turn, Loren skips the night-owl comment or dry-spell observation). The cooldown is 1 turn after any emotional acknowledgment.
